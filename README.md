# Horror Nights

> *The forest is cold, the night lasts only 5 minutes... but the feeling of being hunted will last all night.*

**Horror Nights** is a cooperative survival and psychological horror game developed in **Unreal Engine 5.8**, inspired by the tension of managing resources and resisting day-night cycles against relentless entities. Featuring support for up to **4 players** in a cross-platform environment (Cross-Play between PC and Mobile), the game combines tactical survival, exploration, and the constant fear of what lurks in the fog.

---

## 🌲 Game Overview

In Horror Nights, players always start sheltered in the **Main Cabin** and must gather resources, manage the camp, and survive dynamic time cycles:

* **The Survival Cycle:** Each complete day lasts **10 minutes** (with 5 minutes of sunlight for gathering and preparation, and 5 minutes of total darkness).
* **The Soulless Travelers:** When night falls, dangerous creatures hunt the survivors. The only salvation is finding shelters (cabins, lit campfires, or one of the **6 Special Colored Trees** scattered across the map).
* **The Weather Factor:** The monsters hate water. When it rains, the risk decreases, allowing for risky nighttime outings... *if you trust what you see*.
* **Dangerous Illusions:** The entities can project false illusions (like fake rain or an abandoned car) to lure players into deadly traps.

---

## 👥 Specialized Classes

While any player can perform any task, choosing a specialization is vital for survival in a 4-player team:

* 🔬 **Scientist:** Speeds up workshop production with lower raw material costs.
* 🪖 **Soldier:** The toughest and most proficient in handling heavy weaponry.
* 🔮 **Mage:** Utilizes various support and combat spells.
* 🏃 **Runner:** High mobility for escapes and fast exploration.
* 🍳 **Chef:** Prepares food with greater efficiency and speed.
* 🩺 **Medic:** Specialist in quick heals and reviving fallen allies.
* 🚙 **Pilot:** Masters the driving of transport vehicles with higher speed.
* 🦘 **Jumper:** Extended vertical jump capability for alternative routes.
* 👁️ **Medium:** Sensory ability to detect the presence of monsters.

---

## 🛠️ Core Mechanics & Workshop

* **Dynamic Inventory:** Starts limited to **5 slots**, expandable up to **30** through workshop upgrades.
* **Strategic Crafting:** Build corn plantations, day timers, special tree radars, campfires, engines, pumps, and defense items.
* **Arsenal and Supplies:** Gather wood, alcohol, matches, iron, and food to ensure the group sees the sun rise the next day.

---

## 🚀 Requirements and Technologies

* **Engine:** Unreal Engine 5.8 (utilizing Lumen, Nanite, and pure C++ for maximum optimization and authoritative network architecture).
* **Multiplayer:** Support for Peer-to-Peer / EOS (Epic Online Services) for seamless co-op sessions.
* **Platforms:** PC and Mobile (Cross-Platform).
